Motion study · cell fillPointer

A spreadsheet that puts on a show

Nothing on this sheet ever moves. Only the fill color of each cell changes, a few times a second, and that is enough for wipes, tunnels, pulsing rings and a word spelled out in cells. One cell is special: it follows your pointer, and the patterns form around it.
