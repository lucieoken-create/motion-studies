Light that moves like music

Three presets, like an old music visualizer, each drawn in thin bright lines on a dark screen. Laser beams that cross at one point. Light trails that swirl like a torch waved in a long exposure. A burst of sparks. Scrolling switches between them, and all three gather around your pointer.
